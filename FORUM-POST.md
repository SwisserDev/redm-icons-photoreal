364 inventory icons in one consistent photoreal 3d style, on transparent backgrounds, across 10 categories. Free for everyone, CC0, no credit and no strings. Use them on a paid server, edit them, repackage them, whatever you want.

This is one of 8 styles built from the same catalogue, so you can pick whichever matches your inventory UI.

**Download:** https://github.com/SwisserDev/redm-icons-photoreal

Whole set as a zip on the [release page](https://github.com/SwisserDev/redm-icons-photoreal/releases/tag/v1.0), or grab single files straight out of `png-256/` without downloading anything else.

```
cp -r png-256/*/*.png resources/[rsg]/rsg-inventory/html/images/
```

Filenames follow RSG-Core naming, so most items line up with what your server already has.

## Notes

Every icon goes through the same pass after it is generated: tight alpha crop, uniform fill ratio, centred on a square canvas. That is why a pistol magazine and a burger carry the same weight in a slot instead of jumping around in size.

No real brands anywhere. Where an item would normally reference one, it uses a generic period equivalent.

The icons are AI generated. Saying so up front since people ask.
